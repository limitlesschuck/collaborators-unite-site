The Investment and Commission Structure

Program Fee
$495

Your $495 fee is a prepaid commission credit. It's applied against the first commission you owe on sales closed with matched VIPs. You don't pay the fee and commission separately — the fee comes off the top.

Example

If your agreed commission rate produces $1,200 on a closed sale, you owe $1,200 minus $495 = $705, due by the end of that month. If sales generate less than $495 in commission during the 90-day window, nothing further is due. The fee is non-refundable.

Key Terms

Commission rate: Negotiated individually per coach before the event
Commission window: Nov 18, 2026 to Feb 15, 2027
Payment due: End of the month in which each sale closes
Verification: Self-reported in good faith
